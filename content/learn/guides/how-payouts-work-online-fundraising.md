When you raise funds through an online platform, the donations don't go directly to your bank account in real time. Instead, they're collected by the platform, verified, and then disbursed to you through a scheduled payout process. On Fundraising.ph, payouts are typically processed within 3-7 business days after verification, sent directly to your Philippine bank account. Understanding how this process works helps you plan your campaign timeline and set clear expectations with your beneficiaries and donors.

## The Payout Process

### Step 1: Donations Are Collected

When a donor makes a contribution, the payment is processed through a secure payment gateway. The funds are held by the platform's payment processor — not yet in your bank account.

Payment methods supported on Fundraising.ph include:

- Credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard)
- Online bank transfers (BDO, BPI, Metrobank, and others)
- E-wallets (GCash, Maya)
- Over-the-counter bank deposits

### Step 2: Verification

Before any payout can be made, the platform verifies:

- **Your identity** — Government-issued ID matches the campaign organizer
- **Your bank account** — The account belongs to you (or your organization)
- **Campaign legitimacy** — Documents supporting your campaign have been reviewed
- **Compliance checks** — No flags for fraud, money laundering, or terms of service violations

This verification typically takes 1-3 business days for the first payout. Subsequent payouts are usually faster since your identity is already confirmed.

### Step 3: Payout Processing

Once verified, the platform initiates a bank transfer to your registered account. Processing times depend on:

- **The platform's payout schedule** — Fundraising.ph processes payouts on regular business days
- **Your bank's processing time** — Most Philippine banks credit transfers within 1-2 business days
- **The payment method** — Bank transfers are standard; e-wallet payouts may be faster

### Step 4: Funds Received

You receive the payout in your bank account. At this point, you should:

1. Confirm receipt of the funds
2. Post an update to your campaign donors
3. Begin using the funds for the stated purpose
4. Keep receipts and documentation

### Standard Timeline

| First payout request | After verification | Organizer must complete identity verification |

| Payout processing | 3-5 business days | After verification is complete |

| **Total (first payout)** | **5-10 business days** | From first donation to funds in your account |
| **Total (subsequent payouts)** | **3-7 business days** | Faster after initial verification |

### Expedited Payouts

For urgent medical needs or disaster relief, Fundraising.ph may offer expedited payouts:

- Priority verification queue
- Same-day processing for verified organizers
- Direct coordination with hospitals (medical campaigns)

Contact support to request expedited processing and provide documentation of urgency.

## Bank Transfer Requirements

### Required Information

To receive payouts, you need to provide:

- **Bank name** — BDO, BPI, Metrobank, Landbank, etc.
- **Account name** — Must match the organizer's name or organization name
- **Account number** — The correct account number for the receiving account
- **Account type** — Savings or checking

### Matching Names

The bank account name must match the verified organizer. Accepted scenarios:

| Scenario | Requirement |
|----------|-------------|
| Personal campaign, own account | Account name matches organizer's ID |
| Campaign for family member | Authorization letter from beneficiary + matching account |
| Organizational campaign | Corporate account matches SEC registration |
| Minor beneficiary | Parent or guardian's account with birth certificate |

### Supported Banks

Fundraising.ph supports payouts to most Philippine banks:

**Major Banks:** BDO, BPI, Metrobank, Landbank, PNB, DBP, Security Bank, UnionBank, Chinabank, RCBC, AUB, EastWest Bank

**Digital Banks:** Maya Bank, Gotyme, CIMB, Overseas Filipino Bank, Tonik, Seabank

**E-Wallets:** GCash, Maya (may have lower limits and different processing times)

## Fees and Deductions

### Platform Fees

Fundraising.ph charges a platform fee that covers:

- Payment processing (credit card fees, bank transfer fees)
- Platform operations and maintenance
- Fraud prevention and security
- Customer support

The exact fee structure is displayed during campaign creation and on each donation. Typical fees range from 5-8% of each donation, which is competitive with other fundraising platforms operating in the Philippines.

The exact percentage is displayed transparently on every donation page so donors know exactly how their contribution is allocated.

## Verification Before Payout

### Required for First Payout

Before your first payout is released, you must complete:

1. **Identity verification** — Upload a valid government-issued ID
2. **Bank account verification** — Provide bank account details matching your ID
3. **Campaign documentation** — Upload supporting documents (see [What Documents Do You Need](/learn/guides/what-documents-needed-fundraising))
4. **Selfie verification** — A photo of you holding your ID (may be required for larger amounts)

### Ongoing Monitoring

The platform may request additional verification for:

- Large payouts (over ₱100,000)
- Suspicious donation patterns
- Reports from other users
- Campaigns that have significantly changed from the original description

This protects both organizers and donors from fraud.

## Tax Considerations

### For Individual Organizers (Personal Campaigns)

Donations received for personal needs are generally classified as gifts or donations, not income. Key points:

- **Not subject to income tax** — Donations for personal medical, educational, or emergency needs are not considered taxable income under Philippine tax law
- **No donor's tax** — Individual donations below the threshold are exempt from donor's tax
- **Keep records** — Maintain documentation showing the donations were used for the stated purpose

### For Organizations

- **Registered NGOs** — Must report donations as part of annual financial statements
- **Tax-exempt organizations** — Must maintain BIR accreditation for tax-exempt status
- **Donor's tax exemption** — Donations to accredited NGOs may be exempt from donor's tax
- **Receipting requirements** — Organizations must issue official receipts for donations received

### For Donors

- Individual donations are generally not tax-deductible unless given to a BIR-accredited donee institution
- No tax obligation on the donor for making a contribution
- Corporate donors should consult their accountants for deductibility

### Important Disclaimer

Tax laws change and individual circumstances vary. The information above is general guidance, not tax advice. Consult a qualified tax professional for advice specific to your situation.

## Platform Payout Policies

### Minimum Payout Amount

Fundraising.ph may set a minimum payout threshold to ensure efficient processing. Check the current terms during campaign creation.

### Payout Frequency

- **Standard**: Payouts are processed on scheduled days (typically twice per week)
- **On-request**: Organizers can request a payout when they need funds
- **Automatic**: Some campaigns may be set to automatic payout at regular intervals

### Payout Holds

Payouts may be held if:

- Verification is incomplete
- The campaign has been flagged for review
- There's a dispute about the campaign's legitimacy
- Bank account details are incorrect
- The platform is conducting a routine compliance review

If your payout is held, you'll be notified with instructions on how to resolve the issue.

### Refund Policies

In rare cases where a campaign is found to be fraudulent:

- Donors may be eligible for refunds
- The platform will attempt to recover funds before payout
- If funds have already been disbursed, the platform will work with authorities

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I change my bank account after setup?

Yes, but changes to bank account details will trigger a re-verification process, which may delay your next payout by 1-3 business days. Only change your bank account if necessary.

### What if my bank rejects the transfer?

If a bank transfer fails (wrong account number, closed account, name mismatch), the funds will be returned to the platform and held until you provide correct bank details. Contact support immediately to resolve the issue.

### Can I receive payouts in a currency other than PHP?

Currently, Fundraising.ph processes payouts in Philippine Pesos (PHP) only. International donors' contributions are converted to PHP at the prevailing exchange rate at the time of processing.

### Do I need to pay to create a campaign or receive payouts?

No. Creating a campaign is free. Fees are only deducted from donations received. There are no upfront costs or charges to the organizer.

### What happens to donations if my campaign is suspended?

If a campaign is suspended pending investigation, donations are held by the platform until the review is complete. If the campaign is cleared, payouts proceed normally. If fraud is confirmed, donors are refunded.

### Can someone else receive the payout on my behalf?

Generally, payouts must go to the verified organizer's account. In exceptional circumstances (e.g., the organizer is the patient and unable to manage funds), an authorized representative may be designated with proper documentation.
