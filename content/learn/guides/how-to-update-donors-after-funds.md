The most important thing you can do after receiving donations is to keep your donors informed. Campaigns that post regular updates receive more total donations, more shares, and create lasting relationships with supporters. After donating, your donors are invested in your story — they want to know what happened next. Regular, honest updates honor their generosity and build trust for future campaigns.

## Why Updates Matter

### Donor Retention

A donor who receives updates is significantly more likely to:

- Donate again to your campaign if it's still running
- Share your campaign with their own network
- Donate to future campaigns from other organizers
- Trust online fundraising platforms in general

### Campaign Momentum

Updates keep your campaign visible. When you post an update:

- The campaign reappears in your donors' feeds
- New visitors see an active, ongoing story
- The algorithm favors campaigns with recent activity
- Donors who haven't given yet see that others trust you enough to follow your journey

### Accountability

Updates demonstrate that you're using funds as promised. This is not just ethical — it protects you from misunderstandings and maintains your reputation.

## Update Frequency

### During an Active Campaign

| Timing | Update Type | Purpose |
|--------|------------|---------|
| Within 24 hours of launch | Thank you + initial response | Acknowledge early donors and set the tone |
| Every 3-5 days | Progress update | Share fundraising progress and any developments |
| At milestones (25%, 50%, 75%) | Milestone celebration | Build momentum and thank donors |
| When major events occur | Event update | Surgery completed, funds received, treatment started |
| When the campaign ends | Final thank you + summary | Close the loop with all donors |

### After the Campaign Ends

| Timing | Update Type | Purpose |
|--------|------------|---------|
| Within 1 week | Final update and plan | Confirm payout and next steps |
| 2-4 weeks | Progress on fund use | Show how funds are being spent |
| Monthly (for ongoing needs) | Ongoing progress | Medical treatment updates, education progress |
| When the situation resolves | Final outcome | Share the result — this is what donors are waiting for |

## What to Include in Every Update

Every update should contain these elements:

### 1. Gratitude

Always start by thanking your donors. Be specific:

> "Thank you to the 127 people who have donated so far. Your generosity has raised ₱180,000 toward Maria's chemotherapy."

Not:

> "Thanks everyone."

### 2. Progress Report

Share concrete progress since the last update:

- How much has been raised
- What has been done with the funds
- Any developments in the situation (good or bad)
- Photos when possible

### 3. Next Steps

Tell donors what's coming next:

> "With the funds received so far, Maria will start her first chemotherapy session on Monday. We'll post photos and an update by Tuesday."

### 4. Continued Ask (if the campaign is still active)

If you still need more donations:

> "We've raised ₱180,000 of our ₱650,000 goal. Each chemotherapy cycle costs ₱80,000. Please help us spread the word by sharing this campaign with your network."

## Milestone Updates

Milestone updates are special — they celebrate progress and create momentum. Here's how to handle each one:

### 25% Milestone

This is the "we're getting started" milestone. Focus on:

- Gratitude for the initial support
- What the current amount can fund specifically
- Encouragement to keep sharing

**Example:**

> "We've reached 25% of our goal! ₱162,500 raised from 89 generous donors. This amount covers Maria's first two chemotherapy sessions. The oncology team at PGH has scheduled her first session for next week. Thank you for making this possible! Please continue sharing — we need to keep the momentum going."

### 50% Milestone

The "halfway there" milestone. Focus on:

- The impact already made
- What's left to achieve
- Renewed energy for the final push

### 75% Milestone

The "almost there" milestone. Focus on:

- Excitement about being close to the goal
- Specific remaining needs
- Encouragement for one final push

### 100% Milestone (Goal Reached)

This is the celebration. Focus on:

- Deep gratitude to every donor
- What happens next (treatment schedule, purchase plans, etc.)
- Commitment to continue updating

## Impact Stories

Impact stories go beyond progress updates — they show donors the human result of their generosity.

### How to Write an Impact Story

1. **Describe the before** — What was the situation when the campaign started?
2. **Describe the intervention** — What did the funds enable?
3. **Describe the after** — What's different now?
4. **Connect it to donors** — Make it clear that this happened because of their support

### Sample Impact Story

> "When we started this campaign three months ago, 7-year-old Sofia couldn't attend school because of her dialysis schedule. She was always tired and rarely smiled. Thanks to your donations, Sofia completed her treatment and received a kidney transplant last week at the National Kidney and Transplant Institute. Today, she's recovering well, smiling again, and asking when she can go back to school. Her doctors are optimistic about a full recovery. This happened because of you — every single donation, every share, every prayer made this possible."

### When to Share Impact Stories

- After major milestones (surgery completed, school semester finished)
- When there's a visible, tangible result
- In the final campaign update
- As a follow-up weeks or months after the campaign

## Post-Campaign Reporting

After your campaign ends and all funds have been used (or the situation is resolved), provide a comprehensive final report.

### Financial Summary

| Category | Amount | Status |
|----------|--------|--------|
| Hospital bill | ₱250,000 | Paid (receipt uploaded) |
| Medications | ₱45,000 | Purchased (receipt uploaded) |
| Laboratory tests | ₱12,000 | Completed (receipt uploaded) |
| Follow-up consultations | ₱8,000 | Scheduled |
| **Total raised** | **₱320,000** | |
| **Total spent** | **₱307,000** | |
| **Remaining** | **₱13,000** | Allocated for follow-up visits |

### Outcome Summary

Describe the final outcome:

- What was accomplished
- The current status of the beneficiary
- Any ongoing needs
- Lessons learned

### Thank You Message

End with a heartfelt thank you:

> "From the bottom of our hearts, thank you. When we started this campaign, we were scared and uncertain. Today, because of 234 generous donors, Maria has completed her treatment and is on the road to recovery. You didn't just give money — you gave our family hope. We will never forget your kindness. May God bless each and every one of you."

## Sample Update Formats

### Short Update (For Quick Progress Reports)

```
📅 Update — [Date]

Progress: ₱[amount] raised of ₱[goal] ([percentage]%)

Thank you to everyone who has donated so far! Since our last update:
- [Brief progress point 1]
- [Brief progress point 2]
- [Brief progress point 3]

Next steps: [What's happening next]

Please continue to share our campaign. Every donation and share brings us closer to our goal. 🙏
```

### Medium Update (For Milestone or Event Updates)

```
📅 [Milestone] Update — [Date]

[Celebration or acknowledgment]

Thank you to [number] donors who have contributed [amount]. Here's what your generosity has made possible:

[2-3 paragraphs describing progress and impact]

[Photo description]

What's next:
- [Upcoming event or need]
- [Timeline for next steps]

[Call to action if campaign is still active]
```

### Long Update (For Major Outcomes or Final Reports)

```
📅 Final Update — [Date]

[Opening paragraph summarizing the journey]

When we started this campaign [timeframe] ago, [brief recap of the situation]. Today, [current status].

Progress Summary:
- [Key milestone 1]
- [Key milestone 2]
- [Key milestone 3]
- [Key milestone 4]

Financial Summary:
[Table showing funds raised and how they were used]

[Impact story — 1-2 paragraphs describing the human outcome]

[Photo or video if available]

Words cannot express our gratitude to the [number] donors who made this possible. [Heartfelt thank you message].

[If applicable: plans for any remaining funds or ongoing needs]
```

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What if there's nothing new to report?

It's better to post a short update ("No major changes since our last update, but we wanted to say thank you again and let you know we're thinking of you") than to go silent. Even brief acknowledgments maintain the connection with donors.

### Should I respond to every donor individually?

If possible, yes — at least to the donors who leave comments or messages. For larger campaigns with hundreds of donors, respond to comments and include a general thank-you in your updates.

### What if the situation gets worse?

Be honest. Donors understand that medical treatments don't always go as planned, disaster recovery has setbacks, and life is unpredictable. Share the news honestly, explain what it means for the campaign, and tell donors how they can continue to help.

### What if I have leftover funds?

Explain transparently what you plan to do with excess funds. Options include:
- Returning it to donors (some platforms facilitate this)
- Donating it to a related cause or another campaign
- Using it for follow-up needs related to the original campaign

Always communicate this decision to your donors before acting.

### How long should I keep updating?

Until the situation described in your campaign is resolved and all funds have been accounted for. For medical campaigns, this may be months. For disaster relief, it could be weeks. For education campaigns, until the semester or school year is complete.
